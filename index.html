What is USE-IT?

USE-IT makes free city maps for young travellers, made by locals. No ads, no tourist traps, just honest tips on where to eat, drink, and explore like someone who lives here.

🏷️

100% Non-Commercial

Nobody can pay to be on a USE-IT map. No ads, no sponsored spots, and no commercial traps. Just raw, honest picks.
